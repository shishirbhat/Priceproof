# Car photography — sources and licences

The hero turntable renders real photography wherever a segment or car folder
contains images, and falls back to generated geometry only when a folder is
empty (see `carImages.ts` and `README.md`). The segment photos below were
sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are reused under their Creative Commons
licences. Each entry credits the author and links the source file, which is
what these licences require.

If you replace any of these with your own or a portal's licensed photography,
update or remove the corresponding entry.
